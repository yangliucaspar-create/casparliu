Who We Are

Offtrail Cycles mobile bike workshop providing professional, on-site servicing for individuals, schools, businesses, and cycling events. From routine maintenance to on-location technical support, we keep bikes performing at their best—wherever they're needed.

Built around convenience, reliability, and low-impact transport, Offtrail Cycles supports a more active, healthier way of living—while helping reduce environmental impact through everyday cycling.

Why Choose Offtrail Cycles?

Convenience That Comes to You

Enjoy free call-outs across most Auckland suburbs with our selected service packages. We save you time and hassle by bringing our fully-equipped mobile workshop directly to your doorstep.

Professional-Grade Service On-Site

Our van is a complete workshop on wheels, equipped with brand-new Park Tool master kit, ultrasonic cleaner, air compressor, and comprehensive stock of parts and lubricants for professional repairs every time.

Expertise You Can Trust

Your bike is in skilled hands. Our certified mechanic brings over a decade of experience and holds NZQA qualifications, guaranteeing the highest standard of work.

Transparency & Ongoing Care

We provide and save a detailed Bike Health Report after every service. This record helps you track maintenance and ensures consistent, informed care for your bike's future needs.
